El Niño 2026: How Big Could It Get?

Wednesday, September 23 · 12pm to 1pm ET

WhenWednesday, September 23 · 12pm to 1pm ET
WhereHogan Hall, Room Hogan A, 566 W 114th St (+ Zoom)
HostCenter for Climate Systems Research, Columbia Climate School
FormatBriefing · Public

The quarterly ENSO briefing from the people who make the forecast: how strong this gets, and which regions face drought, floods, and heat next.

Center for Climate Systems Research (Columbia Climate School) ENSO and Climate Quarterly Briefing on the rapidly strengthening 2026 El Niño: the latest ENSO observations and forecasts as it approaches peak intensity, and which regions face heightened risk of drought, heavy rainfall, flooding, heat, and other extremes in the coming seasons. The science companion to Columbia's Beyond the Forecast panel the next day. Open to the public, in person or on Zoom via the event page.
